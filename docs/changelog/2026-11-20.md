### [Bug Fix] A generated `set`/`toJSON` that changed with a new or removed member is Low

- **Files:** `backend/src/analysis/classify.js`, `backend/src/analysis/reclassify.js`, `backend/src/pipeline/run.js`, `backend/src/web/queries.js`, `frontend/lib/apiType.js`, `frontend/components/RolloutView.js`, `frontend/app/reference/[type]/page.js`, `backend/test/rollouts.test.js`, `backend/test/reclassify.test.js`, `docs/architecture/api-surface.md`, `TECH.md`
- **Why:** 16.0.20710 was Medium because `Excel.ChartDataLabel(s).set` and `toJSON` had a new body. The only difference was one more name in their property list, `allowTextToOverflow`, which the same build added. These methods are generated and list every property of their class, so every added or removed property made them Medium.
- **What:** `markSideEffects` lowers such a change to Low and records the members it follows (`follows`). The member change keeps its own severity. The site says why under the change. `reclassifyStored` applies the rule to stored rows.

### [Bug Fix] Documented APIs reached through an object are no longer tagged undocumented

- **Files:** `backend/src/analysis/api-catalog.js`, `backend/src/analysis/reclassify.js`, `backend/src/cli.js`, `backend/test/rollouts.test.js`, `backend/test/reclassify.test.js`, `docs/architecture/api-surface.md`
- **Why:** the types declare `Office.ribbon.requestUpdate` as `Office.Ribbon.requestUpdate` and `Office.context.ui.*` as `Office.UI.*`, so their changes were Low and "undocumented". In 16.0.20710 the body of `Office.ribbon.requestUpdate` changed, which by the rules is Medium.
- **What:** the catalogue keeps the type each variable and property points to; `typedPath` follows it. Requirement sets written as `**Requirement set**: {@link … | RibbonApi 1.1}` are read too. `reclassifyStored` re-tags stored body changes of such APIs as GA and raises their rollouts to Medium. This is the only case where it raises, and it never raises to High.
