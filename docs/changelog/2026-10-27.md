### [Infra] Healthcheck ping and nightly backups

- **Files:** `backend/src/notify/healthcheck.js`, `backend/src/cli.js`, `deploy/backup.sh`,
  `deploy/silentship-backup.service`, `deploy/silentship-backup.timer`, `.env.example`, `docs/SETUP.md`,
  `backend/test/healthcheck.test.js`
- **Why:** nothing warned when the crawler stopped altogether; the database and the archive had no copies
- **What:** `HEALTHCHECK_URL` is pinged after every crawl (`/fail` when a channel failed; a failed ping is only
  logged); `deploy/backup.sh` at 03:17 UTC: `mongodump --archive --gzip`, 14 daily + 8 weekly, rclone to
  `BACKUP_RCLONE_REMOTE`, `git push` of the archive to `ARCHIVE_REMOTE`; restore steps in `SETUP.md`, checked
  locally against a throwaway database
